\## Executive Summary



We propose precision measurements of ultracold neutron (UCN) quantum bouncer energy levels to search for deviations from Newtonian gravity predicted by emergent gravity theories. Our calculations demonstrate \*\*5σ discovery potential\*\* for graviphoton masses m\_ε < 0.05 eV/c² using existing GRANIT experimental precision.



\*\*Key Result:\*\* Maximum energy shift ΔE₁ = 1.27 peV for Yukawa screening length λ\_g = 1 cm, corresponding to \*\*91σ significance\*\* with current δE₁ ≈ 0.014 peV precision.
